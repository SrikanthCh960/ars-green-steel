# ARS Green Steel Content Recovery Plan

Created: 2026-05-31

Sources reviewed:

- `CONTENT_MIGRATION_AUDIT.md`
- `CONTENT_COVERAGE_REPORT.md`

## Purpose

This plan prioritizes missing legacy ARS content so the redesign does not lose business-critical proof, SEO value, enquiry paths, or buyer decision support.

Phases are based on:

- Lead generation value
- Buyer trust impact
- Product decision importance
- SEO/search value
- Corporate credibility
- Implementation effort

Effort scale:

- Small: 0.5-1 day
- Medium: 1-3 days
- Large: 3-6 days
- XL: 1+ week, usually requires data, forms, integrations, or client verification

## Content Verification Needed Before Publishing

The following content should not be published without client confirmation:

- Phone numbers and official email address
- Corporate office and steel plant addresses
- Certificate files and validity
- Award claims and dates
- Green Steel CO2/tree/vehicle equivalence claims
- Dealer data and locator coverage
- Price data source and update process
- Calculator formula and assumptions
- Leadership names, titles, and photos
- Testimonials and client logos
- Career benefits and application routing

## Final Recommendation

Start with **Phase A - Critical Content** before creating additional supporting pages. The current design direction is strong, but the site needs its business-critical proof, tools, product details, and contact pathways restored before it can confidently replace the existing ARS website.
