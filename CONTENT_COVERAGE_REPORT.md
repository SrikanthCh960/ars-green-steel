## Scoring Method

Coverage is estimated by business value, not word count.

- Covered: The current page substantially represents the original business intent.
- Partial: The current page includes the theme, but lacks original detail, proof, tools, data, or conversion depth.
- Missing: The content is not represented on the current page.

### Homepage Gap Summary

The current homepage is strong as a **brand and UX baseline**, but it is not yet complete as a **content migration baseline**.

Main gaps:

- Trust proof is visual and directional, but not yet backed by full client, certificate, award, project, and testimonial content.
- Conversion paths exist as UI concepts, but price, calculator, dealer locator, and enquiry are not functional content experiences yet.
- Product discovery is too shallow for buyers who need grade, size, specification, application, and FAQ detail.
- Legacy SEO content from rod-size pages and blogs is not represented.
- Contact and download content is not yet surfaced strongly enough for procurement and dealer users.

## Cross-Page Content Gaps

These gaps affect both Homepage and About:

| Gap | Business risk | Recommended action |
|---|---|---|
| Legacy trust proof is not fully migrated | Visitors may enjoy the design but still lack enough proof to enquire | Add certificates, awards, clients, testimonials, and projects |
| Conversion tools are conceptual | Users cannot complete price, calculator, dealer, or quote tasks | Build real tool pages and link them from homepage CTAs |
| Product content is shallow | Technical buyers cannot evaluate ARS 550D or CRS properly | Build product detail pages with specs, benefits, FAQs, downloads |
| Rod-size SEO content is absent | Search traffic and buyer education content may be lost | Create size pages or a structured size guide |
| Corporate content is incomplete | Institutional users may not see enough company depth | Expand About and create Leadership, CSR, Careers |
| Contact details are not fully surfaced | Leads may hesitate or fail to convert | Build Contact and Enquiry pages with verified details |
| Sustainability content is only hinted | Green Steel positioning lacks enough proof | Build Green Steel / Certifications content |

## Priority Recommendations

### High Priority

- Add client/project proof to the homepage.
- Build real Steel Price, TMT Calculator, Dealer Locator, and Enquiry destinations.
- Create Product detail pages for ARS 550D and ARS CRS 550D.
- Add Certifications page with verified certificates and awards.
- Add Contact page with verified phone, email, office, and plant details.
- Restore rod-size content through product size pages or a structured size guide.

### Medium Priority

- Expand About with Vision, Mission, MD's Desk, group history, and leadership.
- Create Quality / Testing page with manufacturing and testing process proof.
- Build Green Steel page with verified sustainability claims.
- Add testimonial and client-logo sections after validation.
- Add downloadable brochures and product leaflets.

### Low Priority

- Build full blog archive after core conversion pages are stable.
- Add video gallery once content is verified and curated.
- Add CSR and Careers pages after corporate approval.
- Add privacy/legal pages before public launch.

## Final Assessment

Homepage V1 and About V1 are visually aligned with the modern ARS direction, but they should be treated as **design and UX baselines**, not complete content migration outputs.

Current content readiness:

- Homepage: **42% covered**
- About: **32% covered**

The next work should prioritize business-critical missing content: proof, products, tools, contact/enquiry, certifications, and legacy corporate credibility.
