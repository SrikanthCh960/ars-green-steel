## Final section order and exact content

Place this section after `How it differs from conventional steelmaking` and before `Why lower-carbon steel is being considered`.

1. **Section label:** `Embodied Carbon`
2. **H2:** `Embodied carbon in steel and construction`
3. **Intro:** `Embodied carbon is the emissions associated with producing and bringing construction materials to a project. For steel, the production route, energy inputs, recycled content, transport, and product-specific documentation can all affect the assessment.`
4. **H3:** `Keep general education separate from product proof`
   - Copy: `General information about lower-carbon steel does not establish the embodied-carbon result of an individual product. Project teams should request current, product-specific environmental documentation before using a figure in a project assessment.`
