## Pending-document architecture recommendations

- **Binders:** public name **ARS Binders**; new product page under Products, add to product mega-menu and Product footer column; breadcrumb `Home > Products > ARS Binders`. Use the product-page template. Search intent: factory-made TMT binders/stirrups. Avoid repeating 550D/CRS base-product copy; link to both grades, calculator, dealer locator and quote. Do not build until product availability, specification table, product photographs, brochure and substantiated performance claims are approved.
- **Road / Bridges / Institutions:** retain existing public routes to preserve current URLs. Label the menu group **Solutions > Project types**; show all three there, and list them under Solutions in the footer. Breadcrumb pattern `Home > Solutions > Project Types > [page]`. Reuse a project-type template with source-led hero, engineering rationale, use cases, product proof, FAQ and quote CTA. Search intent is project-specific TMT selection. Avoid duplicating the generic Industries hub and product-specification pages.
- **Vision & Mission:** superseded by the approved standalone `/vision-mission` implementation. The global About navigation and footer point to the standalone page; the conflicting `/about#vision` content was removed. Primary CTA: **Explore Our Products**; secondary CTA: **Contact Our Team**.
- **Leadership messages:** “From the Mouth of Horses” is not a professional public title. Use **Leadership Perspectives** with the section heading **Messages from Our Leadership**. Place as a new section of `/our-team`, not a child page, unless ARS later needs individual long-form executive profiles. Add it to the About mega-menu as a Leadership sub-link, not the footer. Breadcrumb remains `Home > About > Leadership`. Block until ARS confirms the quotations, attribution, titles and portrait usage are approved for public release.
- **Embodied Carbon:** the current `/green-steel` already has an `#embodied-carbon` section and is the appropriate educational parent. Do not add a new route yet. Use `Home > Sustainability > Green Steel > Embodied Carbon` in on-page breadcrumbs/section navigation. CTA: **Explore ARS Green Steel**. Search intent: embodied carbon in construction and lower-carbon steel. Avoid re-explaining “What is Green Steel”; quote only approved emission data and cite its supporting evidence.
- **SGBC:** the user-approved scope supersedes the earlier certificate-library recommendation. The standalone `/sgbc` route uses the source document’s SGBC visible copy and Sustainability navigation label. The page uses the embedded SGBC Leader badge and the local certificate file; ARS must still confirm current rating, certificate scope/date, citation, and external-logo permissions before production publication.
- **What Is Green Steel:** treat as the primary, non-brand explainer on `/green-steel`, while `/ars-green-steel` remains the brand/product proof page and links to it. This avoids competing pages for the same query. The final decision is blocked until the owner selects a canonical content boundary and confirms whether the existing `/green-steel` copy may be replaced.
